# Breakable object reference

The River Crossing trial implements a fence, a crate, and an ordinary barrel. Each uses the same select, confirm, roll, and collision flow. The DCs are encounter settings. The catalog below prioritizes objects by the tactical decisions they could add to the GBA game.

## Baldur’s Gate 3 examples

| Rank | Object | Behavior to study |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Wooden barrel | A small object with its own hit points. [Wooden Barrel](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Wooden_Barrel) |
| 2 | Oil barrel | Breaking the container leaves a flammable surface. [Oil Barrel](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Oil_Barrel) |
| 3 | Smokepowder barrel | Ignition changes the risk of clustering around an object. [Smokepowder Barrel](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Smokepowder_Barrel) |
| 4 | Cracked wall | Damage type and a damage threshold matter. [Goblin Camp entrance](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Defeat_the_Goblins) |
| 5 | Cave-in | Clearing a major obstruction opens an encounter route. [Find the Explosives](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Find_the_Explosives) |
| 6 | Web bridge | Destroying the support drops creatures standing on it. [Phase Spider Matriarch](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Phase_Spider_Matriarch) |
| 7 | Spider egg | Removing a fragile object can prevent a future enemy from appearing. [Phase Spider Matriarch](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Phase_Spider_Matriarch) |
| 8 | Web surface | Fire changes the surface and therefore the movement problem. [Web](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Web_(surface)) |
| 9 | Runepowder barrel | A large blast changes the risk to allies near an obstacle. [Runepowder Barrel](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Runepowder_Barrel) |

## Candidates for our maps

BG3’s container and environmental object catalogs provide visual references for props. The entries below are GBA design candidates. Their proposed interactions are specific to our project. [BG3 containers](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Container) · [BG3 environmental objects](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Category:Environmental_objects)

| --- | --- | --- |

For encounter design, use the cheap props to teach selection and collision. Use surface changes, support removal, and damage thresholds when an encounter gives players a reason to choose one object over another.
