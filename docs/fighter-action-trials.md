# Fighter action trials

Each pair gives the same rule two different targets or situations. Dash and Dodge apply to Alden, so their props provide a reason to use the action. The blue side panel identifies the target. The bottom row shows available commands. Show a short result only when the action resolves.

| Action | First encounter | Second encounter | Rule and animation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attack | Straw dummy | Training skeleton | Resolve the equipped weapon or an Unarmed Strike. Show contact, a miss, or a guarded hit. |
| Dash | Bridge checkpoint | Ruined gate checkpoint | Add Speed to this turn's movement allowance. Use the running map pose and flash the new allowance. |
| Disengage | Spear guard | Wolf | Suppress opportunity attacks from Alden's movement this turn. Show a guarded step out of reach. |
| Dodge | Practice swordsman | Rooftop archer | Apply the Dodge defenses until Alden's next turn, subject to the rule's conditions. Show bracing or ducking. |
| Help | Ally at a stuck winch | Ally fighting a guard | Aid the ally's next qualifying check or first qualifying attack. Show Alden lending a hand or distracting the guard. |
| Hide | Crate stack | Stone alcove | Check cover, sight lines, and Stealth. Show Alden crouching out of view. |
| Ready | Gate sentry | Enemy crossing a bridge | Choose a supported trigger and response. Hold a ready pose and spend the Reaction when the trigger fires and the player accepts. |
| Utilize | Gate lever | Healing kit | Apply the object's own rule. Show the lever throw or bandaging in place. |
| Search | Concealed tripwire | Hidden compartment | Resolve the relevant Wisdom check. Reveal the wire or compartment on success. |
| Study | Defaced heraldry | Arcane inscription | Resolve the relevant Intelligence check. Show a short examination and the clue earned. |
| Influence | Reluctant watchman | Frightened traveler | Resolve the NPC's willingness and any required check. Show a portrait and a short response. |
| Magic | Practice target for a spell | Sealed rune for a magic item | Offer only a spell or magic item the actor can use. Show its cast and effect. |

Dash is implemented as a regular Action. River Crossing also grants a prototype Bonus Action Dash. Existing Smash remains an Athletics interaction. The encounters in the table are the design for subsequent playable trials. Disengage, Dodge, Help, and Ready need enemy or ally turns before their effects can be tested end to end.
